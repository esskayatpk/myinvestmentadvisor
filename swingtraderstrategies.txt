🔵 PART 1 — Swing Trading Starter Strategy (Beginner‑Friendly)

This is the simplest, most repeatable swing trading method for beginners.

1. Trend + Pullback Strategy (Beginner Gold Standard)

This works on stocks, ETFs, crypto, or indices.

Setup Rules

Trade only in clear uptrends or downtrends

Uptrend: price above 20‑EMA & 50‑EMA

Downtrend: price below 20‑EMA & 50‑EMA

Wait for a pullback to the 20‑EMA or a support zone

Enter when price bounces with confirmation (bullish candle, RSI rising, volume uptick)

Entry

Buy on the first strong bullish candle after the pullback

Stop loss: below the swing low

Target: 2R (twice your risk)

Why this works

You’re trading with the trend, not guessing tops/bottoms

Pullbacks give low‑risk entries

You avoid chasing breakouts

Beginner Goal

Execute 30–50 trades with strict rules

Focus on discipline, not profit

🔵 PART 2 — Mature & Proven Swing Trading Strategies

Once you master the starter strategy, you add these two professional‑grade systems.

⭐ Strategy 2: Breakout + Retest (Institutional Favorite)

Setup

Identify a major level:

Resistance, trendline, or consolidation top

Wait for a breakout

DO NOT enter the breakout

Enter on the retest of the breakout level

Entry

Buy when price retests the breakout level and holds

Stop loss: below retest low

Target: 2–3R

Why this works

Institutions accumulate during retests

You avoid false breakouts

High win rate + clean structure

⭐ Strategy 3: RSI Divergence + Trend Reversal (Advanced but powerful)

Setup

Price makes a lower low, RSI makes a higher low → bullish divergence

Or price makes a higher high, RSI makes a lower high → bearish divergence

Combine with support/resistance

Entry

Enter when price confirms with a reversal candle

Stop loss: below divergence low

Target: 2–4R

Why this works

Divergence signals momentum exhaustion

You catch early trend reversals

Fewer trades, but high reward potential

🔵 PART 3 — Risk Management Framework (Non‑Negotiable)

This is where swing traders survive.

Rules

Risk 1% of account per trade

Max 3 open trades

Max 2 losing trades/day

Max 6 losing trades/week

Stop trading for the week if you hit max loss

Position sizing formula

[ \text{Position Size} = \frac{\text{Account Size} \times 0.01}{\text{Stop Loss Distance}} ]

Why this matters

Swing trading profits come from risk control, not prediction accuracy.
